The Challenge

HC Foods imports a diverse portfolio of Asian food products into the US market. With dozens of suppliers across multiple countries and a growing product catalog, managing FSVP compliance had become a full-time job. Their team was buried in spreadsheets, chasing expiring documents, and spending weeks preparing for FDA inspections.

“We were spending 20+ hours a week just on compliance paperwork. Every supplier had a different folder structure, every product had a different spreadsheet. When FDA came for an inspection, we needed three people working for a week to pull everything together.”

— Compliance Manager, HC Foods

The Platform

Four compliance modules. One subscription.

Whether you import food, children’s products, consumer goods, or all three — Aleph covers your compliance needs in a single platform.

FSVP

FDA-required foreign supplier verification for food importers. Supplier profiles, hazard analysis, verification tracking.

CPSIA / CPC

Children’s Product Certificates with lab finder, test tracking, and annual re-test reminders.

Prop 65

California chemical warnings with exposure calculator, safe harbor thresholds, and label generation.

“Forever chemicals” tracking across 15 US states with supplier questionnaire generator.
